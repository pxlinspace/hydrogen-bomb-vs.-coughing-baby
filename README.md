# Hydrogen Bomb vs. Coughing Baby: The Game

A where you play as a coughing baby who is tasked with intercepting an incoming hydrogen bomb before it destroys the city.

This game was made entirely in Godot, and the animated sprites are created in Aseprite.

Motivation: my friend sent me a video of a coughing baby, I was reminded of the meme, I got the game idea, and then I impulsively started on it. That was literally it.

This is a platformer where the baby can cough to propel themselves in the opposite direction! Urgency is at the core of this game, so the controls are built to encourage the player to speedrun as fast as they can!
Not only is movement fast, but the jump is floaty with low gravity, the quickly recharging boosts encourage the player to use them as much as possible, and the speed fall allows the player to hit the ground quicker
so they can jump even earlier.
